SheetKit

Select your cells. Make them work better.

Compares all selected columns, ignoring letter case. Only cells inside the selection move up; cells outside stay in place. Select your whole table to keep rows aligned.

Joins displayed text without physically merging cells. Replaces formulas with their displayed results and clears the other selected contents.

Changes plain text only. Formulas, numbers and dates are skipped. Title case capitalizes each word; sentence case also lowercases names and acronyms. Changed cells lose text links and mixed character styles.
